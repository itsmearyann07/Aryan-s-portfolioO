My Journey

I was always a curious and creative person, but I never knew that creativity could become a career. Since childhood, I was drawn towards drawing, graffiti, dance, hip-hop, beatboxing, B-boying, backflips, and anything that sparked my curiosity. I never thought about where it would lead — I simply loved creating and trying new things.

Then came the pandemic. Because of the lockdown, I had to go to my village. While randomly scrolling through YouTube, I came across an illustration that caught my attention. I remember thinking, “I want to make something like this.” I didn’t know how it was made, but I was determined to learn. I started experimenting every day, often sitting in temples and working on my art. Slowly, it became my escape, my stress buster, and eventually, my passion.

I started posting my work on Instagram, and people began noticing it. I discovered photo manipulation through artists like Benny Productions and started creating on my phone. Later, with my brother’s help, I got a PC and started exploring more — combining illustration with photo manipulation, adding live-action elements, and eventually learning animation.

That curiosity naturally led me into video editing. I wanted to understand everything — storytelling, pacing, music, sound, motion, and the visual language behind a video. I kept learning, experimenting, and working on different kinds of projects until video editing became my profession.

What started with curiosity became a passion.
That passion became a skill.
And eventually, that skill became my profession.

And I’m still following that same curiosity — creating, learning, and seeing where it takes me next.
